Your capacity to care is the starting point.

You do not need to become someone else to begin. Bring the judgment, experience, and wish to be useful that you already carry. We want to equip clinicians—and people whose work is healing, restorative, or stewarding—to learn together and make more good possible.

As a nurse leader would guide a new colleague: begin with one manageable task, protect what has been entrusted to you, ask when uncertain, and review before acting. Read alongside the installation video

The video's “sterile field” is a useful metaphor—not a security or compliance guarantee. Some technical claims require correction:

  • Local Hermes does not mean offline inference. Cloud models, tools, memory services, and integrations may send data outside your device; review all routes.
  • A workspace folder is not a sandbox. Selecting a folder alone does not prove that an agent cannot access other files. Separate permissions and tested controls are required.
  • SOUL readback is not enforced governance. A prompt acknowledgment does not bind an agent to a professional license, verify credentials, or demonstrate runtime enforcement.
  • file:// does not prove network isolation. A local HTML file can still contact external services depending on its code.
  • The equipment example is not a current hardware specification. Check official Hermes requirements; local LLM hardware needs vary.
  • Local files do not guarantee exclusive ownership or privacy. Provider terms, accounts, sync, backups, retention, employer rights, and permissions still matter.

No PHI or confidential institutional data is authorized here. Use the current physician setup guide and official Hermes documentation, preserve existing configuration, and obtain exact-scope approval before changes.

A profile that fits your responsibilities—not just your title

Clinical contributor

Without a management or business role: prepare learning plans, organize public evidence, draft non-patient-specific teaching materials, and structure interdisciplinary discussions for human review.

Clinical or organizational leader

With management responsibilities: prepare meeting agendas, map project dependencies, track explicitly accepted commitments, and surface decisions that remain with authorized humans. A leadership title never grants access automatically.

Practice owner or entrepreneur

With a business: draft a service concept, map nonclinical processes, organize research, and prepare governance questions. Keep business, clinical, and personal contexts separate. No autonomous purchasing, financial, personnel, or patient-care actions.

Knowledge contributor and steward

Review educational resources, attach provenance, document limitations, propose improvements, and maintain shared assets with named owners and review dates. Contribution is optional; publishing and admission require separate review.

Why it fits clinicians who orchestrate and coordinate care

Care coordination depends on connecting people, knowledge, decisions, and follow-through. These are precisely the professional work patterns Nurse AI OS is designed to organize. Its intended value is not replacing clinical expertise, but preparing clearer information, preserving context across handoffs, and making responsibility visible.

  • Personal intelligence: a private space for planning and professional reflection without patient or sensitive personnel details.
  • Shared professional knowledge: attributed resources with owners, versions, and review requirements.
  • Team intelligence: bounded coordination patterns in which access, audiences, and output destinations must be authorized independently.
  • Stewardship: humans evaluate, correct, maintain, and retire resources instead of treating AI output as institutional truth.

These are design goals, not evidence of validated clinical outcomes or complete runtime enforcement. The nurse-led foundation supports interdisciplinary collaboration; it does not give physicians nursing credentials or transfer any clinician’s professional authority to an agent.
