Monthly Quality Check

Once a month, your coordinator and one other person spend 15 minutes answering four questions. If the hub isn't working for your community, this is where you'll see it first, and where you decide what to change.

The four questions

QuestionWhat to look atHealthy signWarning sign
1. Are people using it?New members welcomed, active volunteers, messages openedNumbers steady or growingSign-ups but no follow-through
2. Were the resources right?Spot-check 10 referrals or listingsAll 10 real, current, reachableAny closed, wrong, or outdated
3. Did anything go out that shouldn't have?The approval log and any complaintsEvery message had a named approverAnything sent without approval, or private details shared
4. What couldn't it do?Requests the hub couldn't meetA short list of new ideasThe same unmet need month after month

Decide one change

Pick one thing to change this month, then tell the hub in plain language. For example:

  • "Remove the food pantry on Main Street. It closed."
  • "Add a list of rides to medical appointments."
  • "Send reminders two days before events, not one."

One change this month:

Stop and call for help if

  • A private health detail or personal information went out.
  • Someone in crisis was given AI advice instead of a person.
  • A message went out without approval.

If any of these happen, pause the hub, tell your leadership circle, and review the log before restarting.

Keep each month's check. After 90 days, these sheets are your evidence of what the hub did for your community.
