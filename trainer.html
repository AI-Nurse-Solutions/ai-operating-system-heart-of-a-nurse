What the trainer supports

Practice, review, and aim the next set.

The trainer is built around the work after an answer: reading the rationale, noticing a test-taking pattern, and choosing the next practice session with purpose.

01

Run a session

Choose a timed simulation when you want exam pacing, or use Quick10 for a short, focused check-in.

02

Review the signal

Use rationales and testmanship prompts, then keep missed and flagged questions available for deliberate review.

03

Target the next set

Return for targeted study and follow separate progress for each certification track rather than blending the work.

A seven-day study loop

Make the next week measurable.

Start with a baseline, review what it reveals, then repeat a targeted set before seven days pass. The useful measure is your own completed practice and review—not a prediction of an exam result.

  1. 1Baseline
    Complete one timed simulation or Quick10.
  2. 2Review misses
    Read rationales and flag the questions worth revisiting.
  3. 3Targeted repeat
    Practice a focused set and compare your own activity within seven days.
