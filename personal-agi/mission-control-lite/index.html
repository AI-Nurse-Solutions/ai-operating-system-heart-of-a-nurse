Safety boundary

No PHI. No patient identifiers. No employer secrets. No credentials. This page is a personal, browser-local view of your own governed system. It uploads nothing, activates nothing, and grants no authority. Modules marked planned or doctrine-ready are honest labels — not features pretending to exist.

Ceilings: Green/Yellow · D0/D1 · Observe → Draft → Recommend. Anything wider needs a separately authorized environment.
