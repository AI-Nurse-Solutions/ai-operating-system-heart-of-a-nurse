## The mission
Equip people to scale good with the heart of a nurse: dignity, restoration, honest learning, human judgment, and responsible community contribution. Governance is the method—not a substitute for compassion. Personal AGI is an aspiration, not a claim of achieved AGI.

- dashboard.html: editable professional priorities and review-boundary template.
- mission-control.html: editable one-mission planning and status template.
- SETUP-CHEATSHEET.md: full presetup-to-handoff guide.
- HANDOFF-PROMPT.txt: read-only first-handoff request for Hermes.

- stewardship-ceremony.html: optional offline ceremony and self-generated commemorative certificate.
- AGENT-COMPANION-GUIDE.md: customization, thinking-partner, and Chief-of-Staff guidance.

Open the HTML files locally in a browser after extracting the ZIP. They work offline and have no external libraries, model calls, telemetry, automatic storage, integrations, or agent execution. Export Markdown before closing or refreshing; unsaved notes are lost. Keep exports local and protected. These are manual standalone templates, not Hermes native dashboard or Mission Control plugins. Self-reported statuses are not verification of real work.

Use only public sources and synthetic examples. No PHI, confidential personnel/business records, passwords, or API keys. Roles and authority are unverified. ## Ally stewardship boundaries
Protect customers, employees, and community members. Review business claims, disclose AI use appropriately, and avoid discrimination, manipulation, or promises of success. Stop consequential health, employment, financial, or legal decisions for qualified human review. Allied-health professionals must respect their own scope of practice; this pathway does not prescribe clinical care. Employer approval and actual role authority are separate requirements. No PHI, confidential workplace data, credentials, or private customer/employee records. Personal data control is a practice, not automatic sovereignty: runtime-local does not mean inference-local. Check providers, accounts, sync, backups, retention, and permissions; third-party data ownership cannot be guaranteed. Use public sources and synthetic examples here.
