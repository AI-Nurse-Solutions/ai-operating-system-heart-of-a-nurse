What the prompt asks for

Your thinking comes first.

  1. RecallYou state what you know before AI responds.
  2. ConnectAI organizes only the ideas and approved objectives you provide.
  3. QuestionAI surfaces gaps with teach-back questions—not an answer key.
  4. VerifyYou correct every claim against faculty-approved sources.

This is the expected output structure—not evidence of improved grades or learning outcomes.

Our Intent

Nurse AI OS is here to steward AI into nursing practice.

Nurse AI OS is not a replacement for anything you already belong to. Not your unit, not your school, not your professional organization, not your license.

We are not here to compete with existing nursing programs — we’re here to strengthen them.

We are not here to make you rich. Wealth is not the point; integrity is. If value follows good work, good. It follows — it does not lead.

And we are not here to pull you away from the bedside. The opposite. Your clinical practice is the source of your authority. Without it, none of this means anything.

What we are here to do: steward the arrival of AI into nursing practice, so that it happens with nurses at the table rather than around us — and to show, by how we conduct ourselves, what ethical and responsible influence actually looks like.

Stewardship, not disruption. Influence, not extraction.

No installation required

Use AI to expose your thinking—not replace it.

  1. 1

    Recall before prompting

    Start with what you can explain from memory and the approved sources you are expected to use.

  2. 2

    Use a bounded draft

    Paste the prompt only into a service your school permits. Let it organize or question—not decide, grade, or produce submit-ready work.

  3. 3

    Verify against approved sources

    Correct the output in your own words. Faculty, course materials, and current approved references outrank the AI.

You do the thinking. AI may organize, question, and draft a study aid. It does not decide what is clinically correct, complete graded work, determine competence, or interpret school policy with authority. Agents propose. Humans judge. Nurses steward.

Academic integrity before convenience

Keep the work yours.

Compare every AI-assisted draft with approved course sources, correct it in your own words, and ask faculty when permission is unclear.

Check permission first

Follow your syllabus, instructor guidance, and school AI policy. This page and AI do not decide whether AI use is allowed. If the rules are unclear, ask before using AI on the assignment.

Protect people and records

No patient names, charts, screenshots, or clinical records. Do not enter classmate, faculty, preceptor, staff, or student-identifying information.

Own every submission

Never submit AI output as your own work. Verify sources, disclose use when required, and be able to explain every sentence and decision.
