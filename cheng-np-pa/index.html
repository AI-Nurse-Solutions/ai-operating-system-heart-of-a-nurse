1 · Who we are

Three pieces, one purpose

NAIO Institute is a nonprofit being formed to recommend standards and frameworks for how nurses use AI in healthcare — standards that don't yet exist, so we're stepping up to define them. It will hold Nurse AI OS and organize its federated resources: knowledge bases, tools, and a specialist community.

Nurse AI OS is a governed, nurse-built workspace. The desktop version is a sovereign environment where a nurse's files, memories, and contacts stay close at hand. Nurse Life OS, the app version, is the convenient cloud entry point.

Nurse Intelligence Network (NIN) is the community and courses arm — connected nurse communities by locality and specialty, where nurses learn from each other.

2 · What we believe

Where Nightingale meets neural net.

Nurses have always been the stewards of care; AI doesn't change that, it raises the stakes. We believe nurses should set the direction for AI in healthcare rather than inherit it, and that communities — not individuals or institutions — are the unit we serve. We see ourselves as total stewards of those communities.

Stewardship, not disruption. Influence, not extraction.

6 · Where we're going

Reach beyond the unit

We're not building this to stay in one hospital or one city. We have a tiered plan to contact and interface with the organizations that shape nursing — starting at home with AACN, AONL, ANA\California, the Philippine Nurses Association, and Sutter leadership; then the standards and innovation bodies — ANA, the American Academy of Nursing, ANCC, SONSIEL, the Joint Commission and the regulators now writing AI rules for nursing; then the global stage — WHO's nursing office, ICN and its NP/Advanced Practice Network, UNICEF, UNFPA, MSF, and the implementers whose nurses work in the margins where a sovereign, offline Nurse AI OS matters most.

The seat carries real weight in that plan. The ICN Nurse Practitioner network, the NP programs at UCSF and Samuel Merritt, and the advanced-practice conversations at ANA and AACN all need an advanced-practice voice that isn't ours to borrow — it has to be yours. Because you practice on both the NP and PA sides, you can speak for a wider community than any one credential. Your contributions wouldn't be confined to a product; they'd be carried into the rooms where practice standards for AI are about to be set, with the institute's name and your own behind them.
