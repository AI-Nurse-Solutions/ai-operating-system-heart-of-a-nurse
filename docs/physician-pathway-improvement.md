# Physician pathway — four-pillar improvement loop

Owner: Robert Domondon, publication and mission steward. Hermes proposes and verifies; Robert decides changes to mission, authority, charter, and release scope.

The four pillars are **Knowledge, Judgment, Capability, and Contribution–Stewardship**. The public physician companion pathway remains educational/nonclinical; no monitoring observation establishes clinical safety or evaluated EDENA enforcement.

## Each iteration

| Pillar | Evidence to review | Improvement question | Release gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Current official Hermes support/provider links; package/source parity; clear distinctions between verified facts and aspirations | What became outdated, unclear, unsupported, or difficult to find? | Correct sources and claims before publishing. No invented credentials, outcomes, or AGI capability. |
| Judgment | No-PHI boundaries; human decision gates; self-description disclaimers; voluntary charter acceptance; certificate nonclaims | Where could a reader infer authority or permission we did not grant? | Acceptance/download must not imply clinical authorization, enrollment, or activation. Stop on ambiguity. |
| Capability | Exact-head tests; actual browser flows; download bytes; offline exports; mobile contrast; Pages deployment | Does the promised function work, and is the limit plainly stated? | Report observed results separately from intended behavior. Verify live bytes after deployment. |
| Contribution–Stewardship | Named owner; review dates; public-safe feedback; maintenance and rollback record | What helps people become more capable, restorative, and generous without dependency or hidden disclosure? | Review contributions, attribution, rights, audience, and destination before sharing. Human approval precedes broader scope. |

## Monitoring method
- Daily technical canary: retrieve the four public pathway pages and four public downloads; detect failures and unexpected byte changes against the approved release baseline. No credentials, patient data, identity collection, visitor tracking, or account access.
- Alerts are proposals for investigation, not permission to modify production. Keep prior known-good release evidence; do not silently accept changed bytes.
- At each proposed release: rerun the focused contracts and actual browser checks, review PR findings, require exact-head CI, verify the deployment and live downloads, and intentionally refresh the canary baseline only after human-approved release.
- Weekly human review: use the table above to choose one small improvement. User feedback is voluntary and public-safe; do not solicit patient stories, confidential records, or private SOUL answers. There are no adoption, outcome, download-count, or clinical-impact metrics claimed by this monitoring loop.

## Iteration record template
- Observed issue and source (public URL or public-safe feedback)
- Pillar affected and people who could be helped or harmed
- Proposed change, scope, permissions, and rollback
- Evidence actually tested; remaining unknowns
- Human approval and exact release identity
- Live verification and canary-baseline decision
