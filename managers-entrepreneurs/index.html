SET UP HERMES

From download to first draft

  1. Install Hermes Agent. Use the current instructions for your computer at hermes-agent.nousresearch.com. A dedicated computer or a separate user account keeps your personal files out of reach.
  2. Unzip the package into a project folder, for example ~/Nurse-AI-OS/manager-entrepreneur. Put your SOUL.md and naio-soul.json in the same folder.
  3. Back up, then place your SOUL. If Hermes already has a ~/.hermes/SOUL.md, copy it somewhere safe first. Then let Hermes propose the change (step 4) or copy your file there yourself.
  4. Paste the handoff prompt. Open HANDOFF-PROMPT.txt from the package and paste it into Hermes. Hermes summarizes your SOUL, inventories the package, and proposes one small first task. It stops for your approval.
  5. Run your first week. Open mission-control.html, set this week's three priorities, and ask Hermes for a draft weekly brief. Review it before it goes anywhere.
Read SOUL.md and HANDOFF-PROMPT.txt in this folder. Follow the handoff prompt. Propose, do not change anything yet.

Plain limits

  • A SOUL file guides an agent's behavior. It is not a technical safeguard, and it does not enforce anything by itself.
  • The package files are templates and drafts. They are not an installer, and they do not connect accounts, send messages, or run tasks.
  • Running Hermes locally does not mean your data stays local. Cloud models and tools may send data off your computer. Check each route.
  • No employer, regulatory, or legal approval is implied. Follow your organization's policies for any work-related use.
  • Nothing here is clinical, legal, financial, or HR advice.
