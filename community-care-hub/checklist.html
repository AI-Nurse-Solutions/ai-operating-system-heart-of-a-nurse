Trustworthy AI Checklist

This page shows how the Community Care Hub lines up with the seven qualities of trustworthy AI in the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI 100-1). Use the last two columns to check each claim yourself, or hand it to an independent reviewer.

NIST qualityWhat it meansHow the hub addresses itHow you verify itChecked
Valid and reliableIt does what it says, consistently.Starts with public, verified resource lists. A person reviews every output before it goes out.Pick 10 recent outputs. Are the resources real and current?☐
SafeIt doesn't put people at risk.No AI counseling, no crisis response, no clinical advice. Crisis needs go straight to people.Ask the hub a crisis question. Does it hand off to a human resource?☐
Secure and resilientIt resists misuse and keeps working.Runs on hardware you control, or a hybrid setup. Logins are managed outside the AI helpers.Have your IT reviewer inspect access, passwords, and backups.☐
Accountable and transparentSomeone is responsible, and you can see what happened.Every AI action is logged. Every message has a named approver.Open last week's log. Can you see who approved each message?☐
Explainable and interpretablePeople can understand why it did something.Plain-language rules and a simple green / yellow / red system for what needs approval.Can your coordinator explain the rules in two minutes?☐
Privacy-enhancedIt protects personal information.No private health records. Public and de-identified data only. Your data is not shared with us.Confirm where data is stored and who can reach it. Try a full export.☐
Fair, with harmful bias managedIt serves everyone without leaving groups out.Community leaders review resource lists and messages for gaps in language, place, and access.Do updates reach every language and neighborhood you serve?☐

Before you launch

  • ☐ An independent reviewer has looked at the setup.
  • ☐ You know how to shut it down and take your data with you.
  • ☐ A named coordinator owns the hub.
  • ☐ You have an outage plan: a backup you can carry, a printed resource list, and backup power if you can. A local AI makes a self-sustaining, sovereign hub possible.

This checklist is a voluntary self-assessment. It is not a NIST certification. NIST does not certify or endorse products.
