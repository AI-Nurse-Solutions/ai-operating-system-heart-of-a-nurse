The collaboration doctrine

Allies extend technical capacity without inheriting nursing authority

The ally contributes

Engineering, data, security, infrastructure, automation, product, accessibility, evaluation, teaching, or business-building capability.

The nurse defines

Nursing meaning, professional priorities, workflow reality, acceptable burden, escalation needs, and the consequences for nurses and care.

Authorized humans decide

Clinical, staffing, employment, procurement, compliance, financial, legal, and institutional decisions remain with properly authorized people and organizations.

The venture earns legitimacy

Nurse participation must materially change the product. Attribution, ownership, compensation, dissent, and withdrawal terms are agreed before commercialization.
