# Nurse AI OS — General Practice Workshop Series

Ten distinct 60-minute workshop program outlines for a mixed room: general public,
nurses, professionals, entrepreneurs, and small-business owners.

## What this series is

Nurse AI OS is framed here as a **personal, nurse-governed AI workspace** — a place where
one person keeps their own context, their own rules, and their own record, and uses AI to
think more clearly, organize complexity, learn faster, produce useful work, and make
better-informed decisions.

"Nurse-governed" is not a subject matter. It is a discipline. It comes from a profession
where being confidently wrong is not a bad quarter — it is a person. That discipline
produced three habits worth borrowing, whatever your work is:

| Habit | In plain language |
| --- | --- |
| **Own your context** | The system reads a file *you* wrote about who you are and how you decide, before it answers anything. |
| **Keep the human gate** | The system drafts. You decide. Nothing goes out, gets sent, or gets spent without you. |
| **Keep the ledger** | Decisions, corrections, and boundaries get written down, so context compounds instead of evaporating. |

## Scope boundary (applies to every workshop in this series)

These workshops are **non-clinical**. They contain no patient care, no medical advice, no
EHR or hospital workflow content, no healthcare compliance instruction, and no protected
health information of any kind. Nurses in the room are attending as people and
professionals, not as clinicians on shift. Every exercise uses the participant's own
non-sensitive personal, professional, or business material.

## Suggested bundles

- **Half day (3 hours):** 01 → 02 → 03. The foundation set. Nobody should build on top of a
  workspace they can't govern.
- **Full day (5 hours + breaks):** 01 → 02 → 03 → 05 → 07. Ends with everyone shipping
  something real.
- **Four-week evening series:** 02, 04, 07, 09. Each week produces one artifact the
  participant keeps using.
- **Entrepreneur track:** 02 → 05 → 10 → 09. Context, boundaries, the business, then the
  automation.
- **Student / early-career track:** 02 → 06 → 03 → 07.

## Facilitator conventions used in every outline

**⏸ DEMO PAUSE** — a marked, timed break in the program where the facilitator inserts a
live demonstration. The outline states *what to show* and *what not to show*; it does not
script the software, so the same outline survives product changes. Each workshop has three
demo pauses of roughly five minutes each. If a demo is unavailable, the surrounding
teaching still stands and the time returns to the exercise on either side.

**The reveal.** Each workshop opens with a two-beat promise: a **setup** line, a genuine
pause, then the **reveal**. Say the setup, stop talking, let the room sit in it for two full
seconds, then land the line. Rushing the beat costs the line.

**Everything is done on real material.** No hypothetical case studies. Participants bring
their own actual unfinished work, actual decision, actual task they resent. The room is
quiet more than you expect. That is the point.

**Nobody needs software to attend.** Every exercise works on paper. The demonstrations show
what the workspace does; the takeaways work with or without it.

## Materials checklist (all workshops)

- Index cards and pens for every seat
- A visible countdown timer
- A projected screen for demo pauses
- A one-page handout of that workshop's template (each outline names it)
- A parking-lot sheet for clinical questions, which get redirected, not answered
