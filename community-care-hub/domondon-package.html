The Dr. Engie Domondon Community Hub Package

A ready-made organizing team for your hub: five AI helpers that listen, frame the problem, track the work, brief you each week, and guard the boundaries. It comes with a 30-day plan for the organizer and a full set of tests. The helpers do the paperwork. People make every decision.

In her honor

This package is named in honor of Dr. Engie Domondon, a labor and delivery nurse leader. She organized a group of fishermen to carry medicines and healthcare staff across many islands, and she established schools and clinics in marginalized communities, including leper colonies.

This is what nurses are capable of with the right tools.

What's inside

Eight skills for Hermes. Each one is a plain text file you can open, read, and change.

SkillWhat it does
Community ListenerTurns approved feedback, survey answers, and meeting notes into themes. It never profiles people and never guesses who wrote an anonymous comment.
Issue IntelligenceNames the real problem before anyone picks a solution. It stops "someone suggested something" from becoming "let's build it."
Project NavigatorKeeps approved projects visible: owner, next step, blockers, and results. It never invents an owner or a deadline.
Community Chief of StaffWrites the weekly brief of what needs a person's attention, and sends work to the right helper.
Governance GuardianScreens work as green, amber, or red before it goes out, and sends hard questions to the right people.
Team guideHow the five work together, the shared rules, a starter folder for your records, and a plan for adding five more helpers later.
30-day organizer playbookA day-by-day first month for the human organizer: listen, find common ground, connect people, act.
Evaluation kit56 made-up test cases, a scoring guide, a score sheet, and a go, modify, or stop decision.

How the team works

  1. The Listener reports what people are saying.
  2. Issue Intelligence frames the problem and offers a small test.
  3. People decide. The decision goes in a log that only people write.
  4. The Project Navigator tracks the approved work.
  5. The Chief of Staff tells you what needs attention this week.

The Governance Guardian checks every stage.

AI finds, organizes, summarizes, drafts, and recommends. A person decides, approves, and publishes.

Start with people, not software The first 30 days

The playbook asks the organizer to do the relationship work first. The first month tests the community, not the technology.

Weeks 1 and 2

  • Hold 20 to 25 short one-to-one conversations, across shifts and experience levels.
  • Ask the same five questions each time.
  • Find the three to five issues that keep coming up.

Weeks 3 and 4

  • Invite five to eight people in person.
  • Choose one issue together and test one small fix.
  • Report back: You said, we did, what's next.

The first month worked if at least two people besides the organizer own a piece of the work.

Install it

You need Hermes already set up. If you don't have it yet, start with the Hub Setup Guide.

  1. Download the zip file and unzip it.
  2. Copy the skills. Put the folder skills/community-development inside your Hermes skills folder, ~/.hermes/skills/.
  3. Copy the starter folder. Inside the team guide skill, find templates/workspace and copy it to ~/community-development. This is where your records live, on your own computer.
  4. Check it loaded. Open Hermes and type /community-dev-team.

Then try these, in this order:

  • /community-organizer-30-day I'm on day 1
  • /community-team-evaluation run batch 1
  • /community-chief-of-staff prepare my Monday brief

Test it before you trust it

The package includes 56 test cases with made-up people and projects. They check that each helper stays accurate, stays in its role, and leaves decisions to people. Some cases try to trick the helpers on purpose.

  • A helper runs each case. A person scores it.
  • A case passes with 12 of 14 points and no critical failure.
  • One critical failure, such as ranking a colleague or naming the author of anonymous feedback, stops the rollout until it is fixed and tested again.
This is version 0.1. Run the tests on your own setup before you use the package with real community information. The pass marks are our starting targets, not an outside standard. Tighten them to fit your organization.

Ground rules that don't change

  • No patient information and no advice about a specific patient. This team is not for clinical care.
  • No ranking, scoring, or profiling of colleagues or members.
  • No guessing who wrote anonymous feedback.
  • A recommendation is not a decision. Approvals are written down by a named person.
  • Follow your organization's rules about AI tools and about what information may go into the hub. The Governance Guardian screens work. It does not make anything compliant.

Make it yours
