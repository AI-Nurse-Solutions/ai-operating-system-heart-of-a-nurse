## Mission
Equip clinicians and people with healing, restorative, or stewardship roles to scale good. The nurse-led ethos is dignity, human primacy, honest learning, restoration, and responsible community contribution. Governance is the method of care at scale—not a substitute for compassion.

Personal AGI is explicitly an aspiration, not a claim of achieved artificial general intelligence. The heart of a nurse describes human values shaping the system, not a model conscience or clinical authority.

## Journey

4. Guided setup: hardware check, clean local workspace, Nous/provider choice, cloud/local routing review, and read-only first handoff.
5. Voluntary charter ceremony: a quiet pledge and self-generated Certificate of Stewardship Commitment.
6. Companion guide: ordinary-language conversation, respectful challenge, customization proposals, bounded draft planning, deliberate memory decisions, and a weekly reflection.

## Charter and certificate boundaries
The values charter accompanies, rather than replaces, the existing per-owner governance charter. Acceptance is voluntary and separate from installation consent. The certificate commemorates self-declared acceptance only. It is not credential verification, assessed competence, course completion, attendance verification, professional certification, identity verification, community membership, clinical authority, institutional approval, or evaluated enforcement. No institutional signature, verification seal, public registry, or automatic enrollment exists.

Ceremony name/initials and date remain in page memory until the user chooses a local export. No automatic storage, analytics, model calls, or application submission is included in the new pathway. Ordinary browser/hosting logs may exist. Names are inserted as text, not executable HTML. Edits invalidate the shown certificate; reset and reload clear acceptance.
