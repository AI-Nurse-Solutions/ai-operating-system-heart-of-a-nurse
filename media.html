The verified one-sentence description

What Nurse AI OS is today

Nurse AI OS is a nurse-designed, browser-first kit that helps nurses use ChatGPT or Claude with practical workflows, a personal values-and-boundaries file, and explicit human-review rules—without handing over judgment.

Core browser-first setup and self-customization are $0 and free forever. Hermes is a separate optional desktop runtime. Nurse AI OS is not an EHR, clinical decision-support system, autonomous clinician, certification body, or finished standalone operating system.
