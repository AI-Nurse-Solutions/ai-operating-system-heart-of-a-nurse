The honest product boundary

A governed professional environment, beginning with a practical browser experience.

Available now

Community experience

Browser-local workflow selection, prompts, templates, SOUL files, local folders, and D0/D1 safety practices. No PHI and no external action.

Capability-gated

Tools and orchestration

EDENA, Florence-X, memory, connectors, and action modes operate only where the specific capability is implemented, tested, evidenced, and activated.

High-consequence use

Credentials, institutional use, conformance, PHI eligibility, and high-consequence workflows require separate governance and authorization.
