How it works

Understand first. Then plan. Then act.

One community organizer, five front doors. Every app runs the same five-step loop — so your situation is understood before anything is suggested.

  1. A

    Assess

    Plain questions, one at a time, with "why I'm asking" on each. Never asks what it can infer.

  2. U

    Understand

    A one-screen "here's what's going on": at risk, eligible-but-unenrolled, or stable-but-stretched.

  3. P

    Plan

    A 30/60/90-day checklist ranked by deadline, then dollars saved per hour of your effort.

  4. A

    Act

    "Do it with me": call scripts, letters, and checklists drafted from your answers.

  5. C

    Check in

    A two-question weekly check-in and a running "saved so far" tally. Then we re-plan.

🧭

Navigator

Evidence-based and non-judgmental. Asks before advising, and hands off to licensed professionals when something is out of scope — legal, medical, or crisis.

🤝

Community organizer

Knows the local map — 211, food banks, legal aid, tenant unions, utility help — and connects you to people, not just programs.

📖

Educator

Every step carries a 60-second lesson — how SNAP deductions work, what prior authorization is — so you can do it alone next time.

Non-negotiable

Guardrails

Trust is the asset. It must never be overspent.

  • Honest about what it is. Life Within Reach is an AI tool. It is not a nurse, doctor, lawyer, or any other licensed professional, and it never presents itself as one.
  • Navigation and education only. Never diagnosis, medication dosing, legal representation, or financial advice. Clear escalation to 911, 988, legal aid, and licensed professionals.
  • Rules, not guesses. Eligibility comes from a versioned rules engine with an effective date and a source on every rule — never from an AI's free text. Every result is a screening estimate; the program decides.
  • Private by design. In this preview, answers never leave your browser. Export or erase them any time. Immigration status is never asked.
  • Opt-in advocacy. Your story is shared only if you choose, de-identified by default.
  • No dark patterns. No lead-selling, no upsells, no predatory products. Funded by grants, public contracts, and union or health-system partners — never by your hardship.
  • Equity by design. Plain language, works on a low-end phone, screen-reader friendly. Spanish, Tagalog, Chinese, and Vietnamese are next for the Bay Area pilot.
  • Accountable. Governed under the EDENA framework; every output traceable to a rule, a source, or a human review.
  • A person is always one tap away. Every step has "talk to a person"; trained human volunteers will handle escalations in the pilot.

How it fits

Part of the Nurse AI OS ecosystem

  • NAIO Institute builds and governs the engine.
  • Nurse Intelligence Network (NIN) supplies trained human volunteers who handle escalations, with an "affordability navigator" track in the AI Practitioner certification.
  • FMN tells household stories and produces the lessons inside each app.
  • The Affordability Agenda becomes a living partner: its authors get constituent evidence; the app gets a policy roadmap to track.

Public claim boundary

Life Within Reach is a public preview. It is not a government agency, a law firm, a clinic, or a financial adviser, and it does not submit applications on your behalf.

Dollar figures are estimates from published federal rules (sources and effective dates are shown on every step). States add their own rules, and each program makes the final decision. Policy items come from the New Democrat Coalition's Affordability Agenda and are proposals, not law.

We're looking for a first community pilot partner — a community-benefit program, a tenant union, a parish, or a clinic. Get in touch.
