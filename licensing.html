The durable rule: licensing is artifact-specific

Use the repository license and policy files together with any notice attached to the exact artifact. In this repository, Apache License 2.0 covers source code and CC BY 4.0 covers documentation, playbooks, and curricula. Third-party works, trademarks, services, datasets, and separately published artifacts may have different terms when those terms are actually attached.

No retroactive erasure: software or documentation already distributed under a valid Apache or Creative Commons grant keeps that grant for the covered version. This page does not revoke or narrow prior rights.
