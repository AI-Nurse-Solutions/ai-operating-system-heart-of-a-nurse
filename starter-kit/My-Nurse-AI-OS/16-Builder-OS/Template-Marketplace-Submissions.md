# Sharing What You Build — the Template & Skill Marketplace (submissions)

Built a skill or template that works? You may propose it for review, but a submission is not approval, publication, endorsement, or entry into a governed marketplace. This page describes the current intake boundary and a future review direction.

## What can be submitted

Skills (SKILL.md), templates, prompt packs, and workflow files that are: **no-PHI by construction**, within nursing scope, honestly described, and useful to at least one of the four audiences (students/educators, bedside/APRN, leaders, entrepreneurs).

## Before you submit — the format bar

1. Use the **Governed-Skill-Template** (`05-Templates/Governed-Skill-Template.md`): identify risk tier, data class, action posture, owner, evidence, and review date as separate fields; Yellow+ proposals include the fail-closed self-audit footer.
2. Load the relevant shared header (Academic-Integrity for study skills, Builder-Boundary for business skills) rather than restating boundaries loosely.
3. Run your own **Boundary-Counsel** review first and note the verdict in your submission.
4. Name a real author (you) and a version. Anonymous or unversioned items aren't reviewable.

## How to submit

Email the file to **great.ai.nurses@gmail.com** with subject `MARKETPLACE SUBMISSION: <name>`, or open a pull request against the site repository. Include: what it does in two sentences, which audience, tier, and your Boundary-Counsel verdict.

## What happens next — current status

- No Nurse Steward Council is currently seated or authorized. The council charter is a proposal template, not an operating body.
- A submission by email or pull request is only a proposal. It does not receive a trust label and does not enter a shared shelf automatically.
- Robert Domondon, as the current individual operator, may perform an editorial and safety triage and decide whether to publish a reviewed artifact. Any such decision must name the reviewer, evidence, scope, limitations, license, and date.
- Labels such as `community-reviewed` or `steward-core`, council votes, quarantine authority, and mandatory review cycles are not active unless a future constituted process is separately authorized and evidenced.
- A decline or request for revision may include reasons and a path to correct the proposal; it creates no certification or assurance claim.

## What sharing is not

Publication is **not** endorsement, certification, clinical validation, institutional authorization, or a compliance claim. It means only that the named individual made the documented editorial decision stated for that artifact. Your name stays on your work; improvements by others require an applicable license and attribution.

> Agents propose. Humans judge. Nurses steward.
