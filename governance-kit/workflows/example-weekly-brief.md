# Workflow: Monday morning brief

<!-- EXAMPLE — a Green, draft-only workflow. Low risk, high value,
     and always reviewable. -->

```
Risk tier:               Green
Data class:              D1
Action ceiling:          Draft
Claim type:              None
Evidence requirement:    None (owner's own notes and lists only)
PHI status:              No PHI allowed
Patient-specific:        No
Human review required:   Yes (owner reads and decides priorities)
Nurse-governed review:   No
External-facing intent:  No
Ledger draft required:   No (weekly summary entry only)
Escalation owner:        [owner's name]
```

## Purpose

A brief the owner can read in the elevator: what matters this week, what's due, what's stuck, and one question worth deciding today.

## Steps

| # | Step | Who | Action mode |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Trigger: first session of the week | — | — |
| 2 | Read the task list, calendar notes, and last week's brief | AI | Observe |
| 3 | Draft the brief in SBAR shape, with three proposed priorities clearly labeled for owner decision | AI | Draft |
| 4 | Draft suggested task-list and reminder changes without applying them | AI | Draft |
| 5 | Owner reads, decides priorities, and applies any changes | Human | — |
| 6 | If the owner reorders two weeks running, include the pattern in the next improvement-loop draft | AI | Draft |

## Escalation triggers for this workflow

- Anything that may exceed D1, Green, or Draft → omit it from the brief and flag it separately for human review.
